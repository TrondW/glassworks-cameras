# 503CX (chrome) — reference-derived spec

Sources: Wikimedia Commons 503cx-07…18 (one chrome 503CX + A12 + WLF, studio shots, all faces); KEH listing
225428-5529459 (chrome body only, six views). Body scale: height 92 (top 49 → bottom −43), width 91.
A12: 98 × 92 × 49 (published). Finish: CHROME version — chrome shell edges, black leatherette; the crank is black
on the chrome version too (both photo sets). Never mix black-version parts.

## Proportions (the big one)
- Body is near a cube: side-panel depth / height ≈ 0.88–0.95 (KEH k05 0.88, Commons 09 0.91, 10 0.90);
  top view depth / width ≈ 0.94–0.98 (07, 13, 16). → body shell depth ≈ 84 mm (ours 71.4).
- Mount ring stands ~1.5–2 mm proud of the front face; the bright chrome annulus is its front face, outer R ≈ 41.5
  (nearly tangent to the front frame on all four sides → leatherette survives only as four corners). Inner black
  bayonet R ≈ 33–37.
- Magazine depth ≈ 0.49–0.52 × height → 46–49 ✓.
- WLF footprint covers almost the whole top: ≈ 80 (z) × 78 (x) (ours 65 × 68). Lid starts ~6 mm behind the front.

## Top
- Front chamfer (~45°, ~5 mm) and the whole top surround: leatherette.
- Nameplate on the chamfer: black rounded-rect plate, chrome outline, raised chrome letters (ours: GLASS WORKS).
- Model plates "503CX" on the TOP surface near both side edges, ~4 mm in, centred ~23 mm behind the front:
  black lozenge (rounded ends) ~15 × 2.5 mm, silver letters (bold "503" + smaller "CX").
- Magazine catch: chrome plate set into the magazine's leatherette top at its front edge (reaching the seam), round
  button with a black disc, white "12", concentric rings.

## Front
- Release (camera right, lower): protruding stepped chrome cylinder (~8 mm), knurled band, brass threaded cable
  socket in the tip; red dot index up-left of it.
- Lens release (camera left, lower): chrome domed button in a chrome ring.
- Red dot on the mount ring at top (lens index).

## Right side (crank)
- Raised leatherette side plate with polished chrome edge: from the rear frame along the top to just behind the crank,
  angles up to the top edge at the red index dot, wraps the crank disc; its lower edge runs from under the disc back
  horizontally (~y −8…−10), curves down into a vertical ~10 mm in front of the rear frame and runs to the bottom.
  The rear-bottom strip outside it holds the round signal window (red/white).
- Crank: black. Disc Ø ≈ 45 (thin black rim, LEATHERETTE face), centre ≈ 10 mm forward of body mid, y ≈ +14;
  lever black, from upper-rear to lower-front (diagonal ~40°), ribbed band along its upper edge, small chrome latch
  near the rear end, folded handle at the front-lower end. Red index dot on the plate at the disc top.
- Strap lug: chrome dome button, behind the crank at mid-height (on the plate).
- Pre-release: small black ribbed lever hanging below the plate edge, under the crank disc.

## Left side
- ISO dial near the rear-top: black plate (square-ish, rounded) with printed "⚡ 64 • 100 ISO" (white), black
  notched dial ring, brass slotted centre screw.
- Accessory rail: chrome rounded-rect frame, long horizontal slot, mid-height, front-mid.
- Strap lug: chrome cylinder/dome toward the rear at mid-height.
- Flash connector: round chrome socket with pins, low at the front.

## Bottom
- Leatherette. Quick-coupling plate: black plate with chrome rim, two chrome stripes, tripod socket (chrome bushing),
  four slotted screws; front-centred. Chrome hinge bracket at the rear (magazine hook support) crossing the seam.

## Magazine A12
- Right: folding crank (black, chrome ring), "12" badge, black maker plate (vertical text), film-plane symbol, round
  frame-counter window in a chrome bezel, round red signal window beside it (bottom).
- Left: round chrome film key (black centre, fold-out key); dark slide handle = vertical chrome WIRE LOOP.
- Leatherette on sides, top, back; chrome edges.

## Finder (WLF)
- Folded: leatherette lid in a chrome frame; raised inner panel with chrome outline whose rear edge curves; black
  catch tab at the rear.
- Open: hood side panels black with a fine square-dimple pattern.
