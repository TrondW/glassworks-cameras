# The Rolleiflex 2.8F (K7F) TLR model: dimensions, mechanics & sources

The model is a Franke & Heidecke **Rolleiflex 2.8F (K7F)** (Bay III, 80 mm f/2.8 Planar taking lens + 80 mm f/2.8 Heidosmat viewing lens, Synchro-Compur MXV leaf shutter, coupled Gossen selenium exposure meter with expanding white DoF band, lower-rear ASA/DIN & filter-factor dial, bottom-front 120 supply chamber & Automatik feeler rollers) in `src/cameras//index.js` and `src/cameras//`. Neutral `GLASS WORKS` / `GW` legends replace maker trademarks.

**Axes** (matching `CONTRACT.md` and `SPEC-M6.md`). Millimetres, measured from the **taking lens axis** on the 6×6 film plane:
- `+X` is the photographer's right (crank side).
- `+Y` is up.
- `-Z` is toward the subject.

## Sources, in order of authority
1. **Official Rollei-Werke Franke & Heidecke Factory Instruction Manual (*Rolleiflex 2,8F in Practical Use*, 37 pages)**:
   - Numbered front & top layouts (pp. 2, 4–5), DIN/ASA & filter-factor dial + film-type reminder (`pan`) (pp. 10, 27, 30), peep-window scrolling `f:` + red/green `t` readout (p. 14), expanding white DoF band (pp. 16–17), forward-and-back crank kinematics & double-exposure backward cocking (pp. 10, 19, 26), open-back 120 film loading (pp. 8–9, 20), X/M/V flash sync & self-timer lever (pp. 23–25), and official optical section cutaway with 52° inward sportsfinder focus-check mirror (p. 22).
2. **32 High-Resolution Multi-Angle Kamerastore Studio Photographs** of three verified Rolleiflex 2.8F units (`k7f_a_01..10`, `k7f_b_01..10`, `wf_01..12`): front-left, front-right, rear-right, rear-left, bottom-left, top-down ground glass & focusing knob, open-back interior, rear baffles/Compur blades, front lens macro, and open hood magnifier.
3. **Franke & Heidecke Official Envelope & Optical Specifications**: `112 W × 148 H × 104 D mm` closed (`model Box3: 112.8 × 144.6 × 102.6 mm`), `46.3 mm` vertical Bay III inter-lens pitch, `80.2 mm` calibrated Planar focal length, Bay III Rolleinar 1/2/3 close-up pairs.
